What should a robot remember?

ECoMEM separates maintaining an evidence-grounded account of the past from learning how to act on it. A reusable library represents task-relevant history as explicit concepts. An evidence-based Writer selects and updates these records, while a learned Reader turns them into memory tokens that condition a vision–language–action policy.

A reusable concept library

Four families describe what and where entities are, their states and relations, events and progress, and temporal order. On a real robot, CupSwitch reuses the library to count cup round trips; ScoopPour extends it with a scoop concept to recognize successful scoop-and-pour cycles.
